# Foundations of Investing

A free, four-session family program on investing for teens and young adults (ages 13 to 30).

**Open the app:** https://mtippleroc-prog.github.io/familyfinancialeducation/

| Session | Topic | Ages |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stocks: owning a piece of a business | 13+ |
| 2 | Bonds and interest: money has a price | 14+ |
| 3 | Alternatives and risk: private equity, crypto and hype | 16+ |
| 4 | Taxes: what you keep is what counts | 15+ |

Each session has big ideas, hands-on exercises (compounding, bond prices, leverage, scam spotting, paychecks and capital gains), dinner-table questions, and short media picks with lengths and ages. The app also includes:

- **Practice portfolio:** a pretend $10,000 with simple rules and a family leaderboard, saved in your browser.
- **Companies and market links:** prices, investor pages and SEC filings for familiar companies, plus rates and tax tools.
- **Reading list:** 20 books ordered by age, with page counts and reading time.

Turn on **Leader notes** at the top of the app for the run of show, prep and watch-outs.

## Updating

All text and figures live in `content.js`. Figures are as of November 2026; tax brackets and rates change every year. It's a plain static site (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) with no build step: edit, commit, and GitHub Pages republishes.
